03Control panels

Engineered panels, built to your requirement.

Precision-built for efficiency, durability and compliance with industry standards.

All 8 panel lines
MCC Motor control

MCC (Motor Control Center) panels

Smooth motor starting, monitoring and protection with DOL, star-delta, soft starter and VFD feeders.

APFC Power factor control

APFC (Automatic Power Factor Control) panels

Maintains target power factor automatically, cuts reactive losses and avoids utility penalties.

VFD Variable frequency drives

VFD (Variable Frequency Drive) panels

Speed control for motors, pumps and fans, reducing electrical stress and energy consumption.
