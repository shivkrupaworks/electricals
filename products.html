Electrical control panels

We manufacture and supply a wide range of precision-engineered panels, each designed for maximum efficiency, durability and compliance with industry standards.

MCC
Motor control

MCC (Motor Control Center) panels

Customised motor control centres with DOL, star-delta, soft starter and VFD feeders, engineered for continuous duty and overload protection.

PCC
Power distribution

PCC (Power Control Center) panels

Heavy power distribution panels up to 4000A with high short-circuit withstand capacity, ACB integration and modular compartments.

APFC
Power factor control

APFC (Automatic Power Factor Control) panels

Maintains target power factor automatically, cuts reactive energy losses and avoids utility penalties with multi-stage capacitor banks.

PLC
Automation

PLC & automation panels

Programmable logic controller panels with touch HMI and SCADA integration, designed to automate and sequence industrial processes.

VFD
Variable frequency drives

VFD (Variable Frequency Drive) panels

Panels that automate and modulate motor and pump speed, reducing electrical stress, with built-in thermal ventilation.

AMF
Backup power

AMF & synchronization panels

Auto mains failure and DG synchronization panels for seamless transfer to backup generators and load sharing during grid outages.

BBT
Busbar trunking

Busbar trunking systems

Compact, modular busbar trunking and sandwich busways for safe, high-current distribution across multi-storey buildings and plants.

MDB
Distribution board

Metering & distribution boards (MDB)

Main and sub-distribution boards tailored to client requirements, with certified metering, breaker protection and safety barriers.
