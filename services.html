Services & turnkey solutions

End-to-end electrical solutions for industrial, commercial and infrastructure projects, backed by timely service and dependable support.

01Core offerings

Our expertise

From design and engineering through supply, fabrication, installation, testing and lifetime maintenance.

S/01

Supply of electrical goods

Cables and wires, lighting solutions and electrical equipment: a wide range of electrical components.

  • Copper & armoured cable distribution
  • MCBs, MCCBs, circuit breakers and relays
  • Earthing materials and protection gear
  • Switches, sockets and wiring accessories
S/02

Turnkey fire-fighting systems

End-to-end fire-fighting solutions, from design and installation to maintenance and support.

  • Design & consultation tailored to client needs
  • Installation of fire detection & alarm systems
  • Engineered fire suppression integration
  • Scheduled maintenance and support contracts
S/03

Control panel manufacturing

Custom control panel manufacturing, electrical wiring and system design.

  • MCC, PCC, APFC and MDB panels
  • PLC and VFD panels for automation
  • Busbar trunking and synchronization panels
  • Precise wiring and assembly to standard
S/04

Industrial & commercial installations

Complete electrical execution for industrial, commercial and infrastructure projects.

  • Complete plant cabling and wiring
  • Switchgear and panel erection and anchoring
  • Transformer and feeder terminations
  • Earthing grids and earth resistance checks
S/05

Electrical maintenance & repair

Dependable maintenance that keeps operations smooth, safe and efficient.

  • Preventive panel inspection and cleaning
  • Thermal hot-spot scanning and re-tightening
  • Replacement with genuine branded parts
  • Rapid response to unexpected downtime
S/06

Testing & troubleshooting

Precise testing, calibration and commissioning to industry standards.

  • Testing and troubleshooting of electrical systems
  • Insulation resistance (Megger) and dielectric checks
  • Relay calibration and breaker trip validation
  • Commissioning documentation and handover
S/07

Upgradation & modification

Upgrading existing electrical setups for expanded machinery and capacity.

  • Retrofitting old switchgear with modern breakers
  • APFC capacitor banks to remove PF penalties
  • VFDs added to existing pump and fan lines
  • Busbar expansion and rating enhancement
S/08

Consultancy & on-site support

Engineering consultancy and dedicated on-site support for project engineers.

  • Single-line diagram (SLD) preparation
  • Fault level calculation and breaker sizing
  • Harmonic audits and filter recommendations
  • On-site assistance during installation and trials
S/09

Installation & maintenance

From design and fabrication through layout planning, installation and final Maintenance.

  • Layout planning and base channel positioning
  • Busbar trunking and riser installation
  • Cable trench routing, glanding and termination
  • A complete package for trouble-free operation
